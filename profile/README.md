### 🗺️ Spotter — SnowRunner Minimap

Spotter puts a moving minimap in the corner of your screen while you drive, so you can read the terrain ahead without opening the full map.

- A minimap that rotates and follows your truck
- Real terrain contours taken from the game's own maps
- Pitch and roll gauges that you can move and snap around the minimap
- Built to keep working after game updates

### 📦 Status

Spotter is in development and isn't released yet. When it's ready, you'll find downloads here.

Spotter is free. It's an unofficial fan mod and isn't affiliated with Saber Interactive or Focus Entertainment.
